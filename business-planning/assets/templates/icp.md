# ICP and anti-ICP template

<!-- The ICP describes who the product is for precisely enough that a stranger
     could sort a list of companies or people into "yes" and "no". The anti-ICP
     is mandatory: an ICP without exclusions is a wish, not a profile. Every
     quantitative bound (size, budget, volume) is a tagged input; qualitative
     traits carry the evidence that produced them (interview, public data, or
     labeled assumption). -->
